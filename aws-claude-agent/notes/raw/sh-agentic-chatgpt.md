Title: Selling on ChatGPT

URL Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt

Published Time: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 08:48:00 GMT

Markdown Content:
The ChatGPT channel acts as a discovery-focused referrer platform for customers to find your products. It's active by default for eligible stores, but you can [remove ChatGPT's access to your products through Shopify Catalog](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt#opt-out) in **Sales channels**>**Agentic** in your Shopify admin.

ChatGPT users complete their purchase on your online store checkout in a ChatGPT in-app browser, or in a new tab when customers use ChatGPT web. Because ChatGPT sales happen in your online store checkout, all your customizations, branding, selling strategies, and payment methods are supported.

Review the following considerations for selling on ChatGPT:

*   You don't need to do anything for your products to be discovered by ChatGPT through Shopify Catalog. For more information, refer to [_Shopify Catalog and product discovery for agentic storefronts_](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products).
*   If you [remove ChatGPT's access to your product data through Shopify Catalog](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt#opt-out), then your product data is filtered out of any data that's provided to ChatGPT through Shopify Catalog.
*   ChatGPT is a product discovery channel. Customers using ChatGPT are redirected to buy products in your online store checkout, therefore there's no option to activate or deactivate a ChatGPT direct checkout setting in your Shopify admin.
*   Ensure that your product descriptions include any relevant legal disclosures in the first 6,000 characters.
*   [B2B-only products](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products#b2b-wholesale) aren't supported for agentic storefronts.
*   If you have any products that are included in OpenAI's **Prohibited products and services**, then they don't display in ChatGPT product discovery results. For more information, refer to [Open AI's Commerce policies](https://openai.com/policies/commerce-policies).
*   There are no fees associated with selling in ChatGPT. You pay only your standard payment processing fees.

You can remove Shopify Catalog access for ChatGPT, but your products might still be displayed or referenced by the AI channel through other external discovery and listing methods, such as web crawling and indexing. If you need to completely hide a product from ChatGPT, then refer to [_Hiding products from being discoverable by AI channels_](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products#hiding-product-discoverability).

#### Steps:

1.   From your Shopify admin, go to **Sales channels**>[**Agentic**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/agentic).

2.   Deactivate **Allow Shopify to manage for me**.

3.   Click **ChatGPT**.

4.   Deactivate **Shopify Catalog access**.

5.   Click **Save**.

The customer checkout experience is different for shopping in ChatGPT compared to AI channels that offer direct checkout, such as [Google AI Mode and Gemini](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/google), [Microsoft Copilot](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/microsoft), and [Meta](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/meta). Customers can pay with any of your accepted payment methods on your full online store checkout displayed in a ChatGPT in-app browser, or displayed in a new tab for ChatGPT web.
