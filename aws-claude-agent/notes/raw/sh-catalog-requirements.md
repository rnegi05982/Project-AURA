Title: Requirements for being included in Shopify Catalog

URL Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/requirements

Published Time: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 08:48:00 GMT

Markdown Content:
There are various requirements that your store, your Shopify account, and your products need to meet for Shopify Catalog.

To be automatically included in Shopify Catalog, your store and products must meet the following eligibility requirements:

*   Your store must comply with Shopify's [Terms of Service](https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms) and [Acceptable Use Policy](https://www.shopify.com/legal/aup).
*   Your store must be on the Starter plan or higher, and your store can't be in [private mode](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/password-page). However, if you're on the Agentic plan and haven't yet published your online store to a paid plan, then your products can still be included in Shopify Catalog through [agentic storefronts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts).
*   Your products must have a [title](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details/product-details-page#title-and-description) and at least 1 [product image](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/product-media-types#images).
*   Your products must have a price greater than 0. Free products aren't included in Shopify Catalog.
*   Your products must be published to your [online store](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store), [Hydrogen](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/hydrogen/getting-started), or [Headless](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/custom-storefronts) channels.
*   If you're using Hydrogen or Headless channels, then you must use the [correct format for routes](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/bring-your-own-stack#configure-routes) to be included in Shopify Catalog.
*   If you're using the [Shopify Agentic plan](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/pricing-plans/plans-features/shopify-agentic-plan) and haven't published a Shopify online store, then your products must include the external product URL.
*   Your products must be published with an [identifiable product URL](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details/product-details-page#search-engine-listing).
*   Your products can't have [Unlisted status](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details/product-details-page#unlisted-products) or be [hidden from search engines](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/seo/hide-a-page-from-search-engines).
*   Your products can't contain sensitive content, such as mature content.
*   Your products can't be in any of the [product categories that aren't allowed in Shopify Catalog](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/requirements#product-categories).

To be included in Shopify Catalog, your store must also meet eligibility requirements related to your Shopify account, store activity, and policy standing. The overall standing of your Shopify account is based on a number of factors, and is reviewed over time. Complete the following steps to improve your store's account standing.

### Secure and verify your account

*   [Verify your email address](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/privacy-and-security/account-security/verify-account-email).
*   [Turn on two-step authentication](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/logging-in/secure-sign-in).
*   [Set up a passkey](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/logging-in/passkeys) if available.
*   Complete identity and business verification when prompted.

### Build a consistent and established operating history

*   Maintain a track record of genuine sales to real customers through your usual sales channels.
*   Fulfill orders promptly and accurately.
*   Keep your [chargeback rate](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/chargebacks/chargeback-process#understanding-chargeback-rate) low.
*   Pay your Shopify invoices on time.
*   When using Shopify Payments, ensure your account is in good standing.
*   Resolve any open policy or compliance issues on your account.

If your store isn't eligible for Shopify Catalog, then an ineligibility reason might be displayed alongside **Sources**>**Shopify Catalog** in the **Sales channels**>[**Agentic**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/agentic) section of your Shopify admin. Your products are classified into categories that determine whether a product can be included in Shopify Catalog. The classification is based on your product information, so it can differ from the [product category](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details/product-category) that you set in your Shopify admin. Complete and accurate product information helps Shopify classify your products correctly. For more information, refer to [optimizing your products for Shopify Catalog](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/optimizing-products).

Products in certain categories aren't included in Shopify Catalog, such as products that are age-restricted or regulated in many regions, or products that aren't suited to the shopping experiences that use Shopify Catalog. A category that isn't included also covers all of its subcategories.

The types of products that are ineligible for Shopify Catalog can change over time at Shopify's discretion. Products that Shopify classifies in the following categories aren't allowed in Shopify Catalog, including the listed examples for each category:

*   **Alcohol**: Alcoholic beverages, and homebrewing and winemaking supplies.
*   **Tobacco and vaping products**: All tobacco products, including chewing tobacco, cigarettes, cigars, vaporizers, electronic cigarettes, and e-liquid.
*   **Drugs and smoking accessories**: Cannabidiol (CBD) supplements, smoking accessories, and rolling papers.
*   **Prescription and medical products**: All business and industrial medical and dentistry products, hearing aids, respiratory care equipment such as continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) machines, tests for human immunodeficiency virus (HIV), and prenatal heart rate monitors.
*   **Weapons, ammunition, and weapon accessories**: Firearms, ammunition, combat knives, swords, stun guns, and pepper spray.
*   **Recreational and imitation weapons**: All hunting and shooting products, including archery, airsoft, and paintball equipment, and also hunting and survival knives, weapon scopes and sights, collectible weapons, and toy weapons.
*   **Explosives**: Fireworks, firecrackers, and sparklers.
*   **Science and laboratory products, and hazardous materials**: All science and laboratory products, including laboratory equipment, supplies, specimens, chemicals, and dissection kits, and also fuels, pesticides, herbicides, photographic chemicals, building chemicals such as drain and masonry cleaners, fire extinguishers, and firewood.
*   **Adult and mature content**: All products in the Mature category, including pornography, erotic videos, erotic magazines, erotic literature, sex toys, and erotic clothing.
*   **Sexual health and wellness products**: Condoms, spermicides, and personal lubricants.
*   **Money-related products**: Bullion, collectible coins, collectible banknotes, and virtual currency.
*   **Prop money**: Play money and replica currency.
*   **Services and product add-ons**: All services and product add-ons, including professional, personal, home, and automotive services, extended warranties, installation services, maintenance plans, subscription services, and finance and insurance products.
*   **Event tickets**: Tickets to concerts, sports, and other events.
*   **Live animals**: Live animals, and animal husbandry products.
*   **Uncategorized products**: Products that Shopify classifies as Uncategorized.

A product can also be excluded from Shopify Catalog when its images or description contain sensitive content, even if the product's not included in a category from the exclusion list.
