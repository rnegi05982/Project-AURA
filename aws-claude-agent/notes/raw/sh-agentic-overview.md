Title: Shopify agentic storefronts

URL Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts

Published Time: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 08:48:00 GMT

Markdown Content:
Shopify's agentic storefronts lets customers discover and purchase your products in AI channels, such as ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, and Meta. Agentic storefronts is active by default for eligible stores.

Your products are [made available to AI channels through Shopify Catalog](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products) or through the [Google & YouTube sales channel](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/marketplaces/google) or Google Merchant Center for Google AI Mode and Gemini. You can [manage which AI channels you want to sell your products on](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-home#manage-for-me) in **Sales channels**>**Agentic** in your Shopify admin.

Your [knowledge base](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/knowledge-base) holds the non-product information about your store, such as your return process, shipping timelines, and brand values, so that AI agents can answer customer questions about your business.

Using agentic storefronts means that your products can be discovered more widely by customers shopping in AI channels, and you get insights about the search and sales performance of your products.

The Agentic plan lets you set up agentic storefronts and keep your existing online store. Agentic storefronts setup is different with the Agentic plan, and direct checkout is deactivated by default. To learn more, refer to [_Setting up agentic storefronts for stores on the Agentic plan_](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-plan-setup).

Review the following details about how selling works for different AI channels with Shopify:

*   [ChatGPT](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt) acts as a discovery-focused referrer platform for customers to find your products. ChatGPT users complete their purchase on your online store checkout in a ChatGPT in-app browser, or in a new tab when customers use ChatGPT web.
*   For [Google AI Mode and Gemini](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/google), [Microsoft Copilot](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/microsoft), and [Meta surfaces such as Muse](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/meta), if you have direct checkout activated, then customers can complete their purchases in the channel's Shopify-powered direct checkout without having to leave the AI channel.

Customers can also pay with Shop Pay when they shop through personal AI agents, such as Muse and [other agents that they connect to Shop](https://help.shop.app/en/shop/shopping/personal-agents). You don't need to activate Shop Pay specifically for AI agents. Orders that customers place through personal AI agents display in your Shopify admin with attribution to the agent.

When a user has a conversation with an AI agent and prompts it for information, ideas, or recommendations for a particular product, the AI agent can return suggestions for what products might suit their needs. For example, a user might say "I'm looking for recommendations for navy running shorts". The AI agent can then search for appropriate recommendations and display a selection of products that it thinks fit the user's prompt.

For orders that are placed through AI channels, you retain full ownership of the customer relationship and post-purchase experience. Orders from AI channels display in your Shopify admin with channel or referrer attribution so that you know where the order originated from.
